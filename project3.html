Camera Controls

  • W: Move Forward
  • S: Move Backward
  • A: Strafe Left
  • D: Strafe Right
  • Space: Move Up
  • Shift: Move Down
  • Arrow Up: Look Up
  • Arrow Down: Look Down
  • Arrow Left: Turn Left
  • Arrow Right: Turn Right
  • Mouse Drag: Move altar cube around
  • Toggle Projection: Switch between Perspective and Orthographic View
